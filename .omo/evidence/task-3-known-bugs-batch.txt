--- DEVIATION FROM PLAN (accepted, see final report) -------------------
Plan said "remove the last_poll_attempt line" (singular). In reality
BackfillDiagnostic carried THREE fields — last_poll_attempt,
last_poll_outcome, backoff_window — and the sentinel rendered ALL THREE
from the same dead `diag` object. Deleting the class necessarily deletes
all three render lines; keeping the other two was impossible (they read
attributes of a deleted type) and pointless (equally never-written, so
they permanently printed 'not attempted' / 'none'). All non-diag sentinel
lines were preserved exactly.
